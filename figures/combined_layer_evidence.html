Layers 7–9: two independent methods, the same answer

A hand-picked single axis and a properly regularized multivariate probe were derived through completely different procedures, on the same 14-binder screen set, then both tested against the same 4 held-out binders from a separate experiment. They agree: the real, reproducible signal for binding activity concentrates in layers 7–9 of the joint (binder+target) embedding.
