Where does "binding" live inside the model?

A single component — layer 9, principal component 7 of the joint (binder+target) embedding — separates real high-activity binders from the rest with near-perfect accuracy, and this same axis was independently confirmed on a held-out experiment. This map shows how that signal is distributed across every layer and every one of the first 20 components, to see where it concentrates.
